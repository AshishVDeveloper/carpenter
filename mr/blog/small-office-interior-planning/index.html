Planning a small office interior: layout, storage and comfort

How to plan a small office interior in Mumbai — workstation sizes, meeting space, storage, wiring and acoustics — without wasting expensive floor area.

Offices & shops2 min read17 Nov 2026By Mumbai Interior team

This article is in English.

In this article
  1. Start with how people work
  2. Workstation sizes
  3. Meeting and cabin space
  4. स्टोरेज
  5. Wiring and data
  6. Comfort and acoustics
  7. Work timing

Office space in Mumbai is expensive, so every square foot must earn its keep. A good small office fits the team comfortably, impresses visitors and is easy to change as the business grows.

Start with how people work

Count how many people sit at desks every day, how many visit, and how often you meet. A team that is on the phone all day needs different space from a design studio that spreads out drawings.

Workstation sizes

WorkstationTypical size
Compact linear deskabout 4 × 2 ft per person
Standard deskabout 5 × 2.5 ft per person
L-shaped manager tableabout 5 × 5 ft with return
Walkway between rowsat least 3 ft

Linear workstations in rows use space most efficiently. Add low partitions with a pin-up board for privacy without blocking light.

Meeting and cabin space

  • A small meeting room for 4–6 people is more useful than a large conference room used once a month.
  • Glass partitions keep cabins private but let daylight reach the whole office.
  • A reception counter with a branded wall creates a good first impression even in a small space.

स्टोरेज

  1. Mobile pedestals (drawer units on wheels) under each desk.
  2. Full-height storage walls along one side for files and supplies.
  3. A lockable cabinet for documents and valuables.

Wiring and data

Plan power, LAN and charging points at every desk before the furniture is made, with cable trays inside workstations. Retrofitting wires later is messy and costly.

Keep the server or router area ventilated and accessible — it should not be inside a closed cabinet with no air.

Comfort and acoustics

  • Ergonomic chairs matter more than expensive desks.
  • Acoustic panels or fabric pin boards reduce echo in rooms with hard floors and glass.
  • Neutral white light (around 4000K) is good for work areas.

Work timing

Office fit-outs often have to be done at night or on weekends in commercial buildings. Agree on work hours with the building management in advance.
