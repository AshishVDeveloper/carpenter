About Mumbai Interior

Mumbai Interior is a team of carpenters and interior workers serving Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Uttar Pradesh. We take on complete interior contracts and labour-basis work for homes, offices, shops, showrooms and malls, and we make furniture to measure in our own workshop.

A family of carpenters

Mumbai Interior is run by the Vishwakarma family and grew out of hands-on carpentry — sliding aluminium window, teakwood furniture, sofa, kapat. Today we take on complete interior contracts as well as labour-basis work, and we still make furniture in our own workshop.

  • Our own carpenters on every job
  • Clear contract or labour-basis pricing
  • Free site visit and measurement
  • Teams in Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Uttar Pradesh
