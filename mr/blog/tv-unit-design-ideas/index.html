TV unit design ideas for modern living rooms

TV unit ideas for Indian living rooms: wall-mounted units, panelling, storage, the right TV height and viewing distance, and hiding wires.

Design ideas2 min read17 Nov 2026By Mumbai Interior team

This article is in English.

In this article
  1. Choose the style
  2. Get the height right
  3. Viewing distance
  4. Plan wires and power before the work starts
  5. Storage that is actually useful
  6. Lighting

The TV wall is the focus of most living rooms. A well designed TV unit hides wires, stores the set-top box and router, and makes the whole room look finished. Here are ideas and practical rules.

Choose the style

  • Floating (wall-mounted) unit: a low cabinet fixed to the wall with space below. Looks light and makes cleaning easy.
  • Full wall panelling: a laminate, veneer or fluted panel behind the TV with a cabinet below. Gives a premium, hotel-like feel.
  • TV unit with shelves: open shelves or a glass cabinet on one or both sides for books and showpieces.
  • Partition unit: in open-plan homes, a TV unit can double as a divider between living and dining.

Get the height right

The centre of the TV screen should be roughly at eye level when seated — for most sofas that is about 40 to 45 inches from the floor. Mounting the TV too high is the most common mistake and strains the neck.

Viewing distance

TV sizeComfortable distance (approx.)
43 inch5.5 to 7 ft
55 inch7 to 9 ft
65 inch8 to 10 ft

Plan wires and power before the work starts

  1. Ask the electrician to put power and HDMI/antenna points behind the TV position, not at the floor level.
  2. Run a conduit pipe from the TV to the cabinet below so wires stay hidden.
  3. Keep ventilation holes in the cabinet for the set-top box and router.
  4. Add a plug point inside the cabinet for devices.

Decide the TV size before panelling. The panel and cut-outs are planned around it.

Storage that is actually useful

  • Drawers for remote controls, chargers and documents.
  • A flap-down or open section for the set-top box (so the remote signal reaches it).
  • Closed cabinets for things you do not want on display.

Lighting

Warm profile lights behind the panel or under the floating unit create a soft glow and reduce eye strain at night. Read our lighting basics for more.
