Questions people ask

Still unsure? Call 9004200401 — we are happy to explain.

How do you decide the final price?

The prices on this website are estimates. We give you a final quote after a site visit, once we know the exact size, wood or board, hardware and finish you want.

What is the difference between contract basis and labour basis?

On contract basis we supply the material and do the work for one agreed price. On labour basis you buy the material and pay us only for the work.

Which areas do you work in?

Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Mira Bhayandar in Maharashtra, and Kunda (Pratapgarh) and Prayagraj in Uttar Pradesh.

Do you visit the site before quoting?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us on 9004200401 to book a site visit and measurement.

Can you make furniture in my size and wood?

Yes. Every piece on this website can be made to your size in teak, sheesham or engineered wood, with the finish you choose.
