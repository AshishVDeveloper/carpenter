What we do

From a single wardrobe to a complete 2BHK, we plan, build and finish home interiors with our own carpenters. We work in new possessions and lived-in flats across Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai, and keep the site clean while we work.

  • Full flat interiors for 1, 2 and 3 BHK
  • Wardrobes, lofts and storage beds
  • TV units and wall panelling
  • False ceiling and cove lighting
  • Pooja units and shoe racks
  • Sliding aluminium windows
