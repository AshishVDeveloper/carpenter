How to choose the right sofa: size, foam, fabric and frame

A practical sofa buying guide for Indian homes: measuring the room, seat depth, foam density, fabrics that suit Mumbai weather and frame quality.

Furniture buying2 min read17 Nov 2026By Mumbai Interior team

This article is in English.

In this article

  2. Comfort: seat depth and height
  3. Foam and cushions
  4. Fabric for Mumbai weather
  5. The frame
  6. Ready-made or made to order?

A sofa is one of the most used pieces of furniture in any home, and one of the hardest to judge in a shop. It can look perfect and still feel uncomfortable after a month. Here is what to check before you buy or order one.

Start with the room

  1. Measure the wall where the sofa will go and the walking space in front of it. Leave at least 2.5 to 3 feet between the sofa and the TV unit or coffee table for easy movement.
  2. Measure the lift, staircase and main door. Many large sofas cannot reach upper floors in older buildings. Sofas made to order can be built in parts that join inside the home.
  3. Decide the shape: a 3-seater suits most living rooms; an L-shape gives more seating for the same wall length but needs a corner.

Comfort: seat depth and height

MeasurementComfortable range
Seat height from floorabout 16–18 inches
Seat depthabout 20–24 inches
Back heightabout 30–36 inches overall
Armrest heightabout 22–26 inches

Shorter people prefer a shallower seat; if you like to sit cross-legged or lie down, go deeper. Always sit on a sample for a few minutes, not a few seconds.

Foam and cushions

The foam decides whether the sofa stays comfortable for years or sinks in months.

  • Ask for high-density foam in the seat. Seat foam around 32–40 kg/m³ density is common for good sofas.
  • Fibre or feather-filled back cushions feel softer and can be fluffed.
  • Removable cushion covers with zips make cleaning much easier.

Fabric for Mumbai weather

  • Cotton blends and linen look fabrics breathe well in humid weather.
  • Velvet looks rich but attracts dust; choose a performance velvet that is easier to clean.
  • Leatherette is easy to wipe but can feel hot and sticky in summer and may peel after a few years.
  • With children or pets, choose a darker or textured fabric and ask for stain-resistant treatment.

Ask for a fabric sample to take home. Colours look different under home lights than in a showroom.

The frame

Look under the cushions and at the base. A good sofa has a solid wood or strong plywood frame, firm joints and a webbing or spring base. Avoid frames that creak or wobble when you sit at the edge.

Ready-made or made to order?

Ready-made sofas are quick, but sizes are fixed. A sofa made to order fits your wall exactly, lets you choose the seat depth and fabric, and can be built in parts for difficult staircases. See our sofa डिझाइन for estimated prices, or ask for a quote.
