प्लायवूड, MDF की भक्कम लाकूड: फर्निचरसाठी मटेरियल कसे निवडावे

Which board or wood to choose for wardrobes, kitchens, beds and sofas in Mumbai's humid climate — BWP, BWR, MDF, teak and sheesham explained.

हे मार्गदर्शक इंग्रजीत आहे.

Mumbai's humidity is hard on furniture. Choosing the right material for each piece matters more than any design detail.

Plywood

  • BWP (boiling water proof) plywood: best for kitchen sink units and bathroom vanities.
  • BWR (boiling water resistant) plywood: good for wardrobes, beds and TV units.
  • Commercial / MR plywood: cheaper, only for dry areas — not recommended for kitchens.

MDF and particle board

MDF is smooth and good for painted or PU shutters, but it swells if it gets wet. Particle board is the cheapest and is used in ready-made furniture; we avoid it for kitchens and wardrobes.

Solid wood

  • Teak (sagwan): strong, resists termites and moisture, and ages well — ideal for sofas, beds and doors. It is the most expensive.
  • Sheesham: hard and handsome with a strong grain — good for beds, dining sets and chairs at a lower price than teak.
  • Solid wood should be properly seasoned, or it can crack and warp.
