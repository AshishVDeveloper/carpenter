Monsoon care for wooden furniture in Mumbai

How Mumbai monsoon humidity affects wood and plywood furniture, and simple steps to stop swelling, fungus, sticking doors and bad smells.

Care & maintenance2 min read17 Nov 2026By Mumbai Interior team

  2. Before the rains start
  3. During the monsoon
  4. If doors start sticking

From June to September, humidity in Mumbai often stays above 80 percent. Wood and plywood absorb moisture from the air, and that is when doors start sticking, drawers jam and a musty smell appears inside wardrobes. A little care before and during the rains prevents most of these problems.

Why furniture suffers in the monsoon

Wood is a natural material. It takes in moisture when the air is wet and releases it when the air is dry. This makes solid wood swell slightly across the grain, which is why a shutter that closed easily in March can rub in July. Plywood and MDF are more stable, but their edges absorb water quickly if the edge banding is damaged.

Before the rains start

  • Check the edges. Look at the bottom edges of wardrobe and kitchen shutters. If the edge banding is peeling, get it re-pasted now.
  • Polish solid wood. A fresh coat of melamine or PU polish seals the surface. Do it in April or May, when the wood is dry.
  • Move furniture slightly away from walls. Keep a gap of about 1–2 inches, especially on outer walls that get wet. Damp walls are the biggest cause of fungus behind wardrobes.
  • Fix leaks first. If a wall gets damp every year, no polish will save the wardrobe against it. Get the waterproofing done.

During the monsoon

  1. Open wardrobes for a few minutes every day so air can move inside.
  2. Put silica gel packets or moisture absorbers in wardrobes and drawers and replace them when full.
  3. Keep naphthalene balls or neem leaves away from direct contact with clothes, but inside the wardrobe to discourage insects.
  4. Wipe up water immediately — from wet umbrellas, plants and mopping — before it reaches the furniture legs.
  5. Run a fan or dehumidifier in closed rooms for a while each day.

Never wipe wooden furniture with a dripping wet cloth in the monsoon. Use a damp, well-wrung cloth, then a dry one.

If doors start sticking

Do not plane or cut a sticking door during the rains. Once the dry season comes, the wood shrinks back and the door will have a gap. Instead, ask a carpenter to adjust the hinges slightly. Most modern hinges have adjustment screws for exactly this.

If you see white spots or fungus

Wipe the area with a cloth dipped in a mild solution of white vinegar and water, then dry it fully and keep the area ventilated. If the fungus keeps coming back, the cause is usually a damp wall behind the furniture.

Choose the right material next time

If you are building new furniture, ask for:

WhereBetter choice
Kitchen sink and bathroom vanityBWP (boiling water proof) plywood
Wardrobes on outer wallsBWR plywood with a laminate on the back side too
All shuttersEdge banding on all four edges, applied by machine

Our plywood, MDF and solid wood guide explains the grades in more detail.

With these habits, good furniture easily lasts many Mumbai monsoons.
