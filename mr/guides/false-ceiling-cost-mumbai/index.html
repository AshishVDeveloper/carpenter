मुंबईत फॉल्स सीलिंगचा खर्च: जिप्सम की POP

False ceiling prices per sq ft in Mumbai and Thane, gypsum vs POP, and what to plan for lights and AC.

हे मार्गदर्शक इंग्रजीत आहे.

A false ceiling in Mumbai usually costs ₹90 to ₹160 per square foot of ceiling area, depending on the material and the design.

प्रकारApprox. cost per sq ft
Gypsum board, simple flat or border₹90 – ₹120
Gypsum with cove / profile lighting₹110 – ₹150
POP (plaster of Paris) design₹100 – ₹160
Wooden or PVC panels (partial)₹200 – ₹400

Gypsum or POP?

  • Gypsum boards are factory-made, quick to install and give a clean finish — the common choice today.
  • POP is made on site, allows curved designs, but takes longer and creates more dust.
  • Both need a good painter for a smooth finish.

Plan these before work starts

  • Position of lights, fans and AC units.
  • Cove lighting and profile lights.
  • Access panels for AC piping and wiring.

Lights, wiring and painting are usually priced separately. Ask us for a complete estimate for your room.
